# The Byrd & CO Lending Marketplace — How It Works

**A modern, transparent way to place commercial real estate loans.**

Byrd & CO connects sponsors seeking commercial real estate financing with active
CRE lenders across banks, credit unions, private lenders, agency, bridge, hard
money, life companies, and CMBS. Everything runs through one platform —
document handling, term sheets, and closings — with white-glove broker
oversight from Wayne Byrd and Caleb Byrd on every deal.

---

## For Lenders

### Why join our marketplace?

- **Only deals that fit your credit box.** You tell us your parameters once
  (property types, geography, loan size, LTV, DSCR, debt yield, rate range,
  recourse preference, decision speed). We only send you deals that match.
- **No cold calling.** Every deal comes with the sponsor's full package
  already assembled — rent roll, T-12, PFS, tax returns, business plan,
  proforma — so you can quote quickly.
- **Structured term sheets, side-by-side.** No more chasing PDFs across
  inboxes. Every term sheet you submit is captured with the full 17-field
  structure lenders expect: rate type + rate, LTV/LTC, amortization, term,
  IO, recourse, prepay, orig/exit fees, expiration, contingencies, and
  notes. Broker can accept, counter, or pass — and everyone sees the same
  data.
- **Direct broker feedback.** When your term sheet is accepted, countered,
  or passed on, you get an email with the broker's note. No mystery.

### The lender flow

**Step 1 — Apply.**
Visit **byrd-co.com/lenders/apply** and fill out your credit box. Takes about
5 minutes. You'll get a confirmation email immediately.

**Step 2 — Get approved.**
A broker at Byrd & CO reviews every application. Once approved (usually within
a business day) you get an activation email with a link to set your password.

**Step 3 — Log in to your lender portal.**
Three tabs:
- **My Credit Box** — edit any parameter any time. Changes take effect
  immediately for future deal matches.
- **Active Invites** — deals shared with you, each with a "View Deal Package"
  button that opens the full sponsor package (property + loan + financials +
  sponsors + docs).
- **My Term Sheets** — every term sheet you've ever submitted, with status
  (submitted, accepted, countered, passed) and any broker notes.

**Step 4 — Submit term sheets.**
Click **Submit Term Sheet** on any active invite. Fill out the structured
form. Broker gets emailed the moment you submit + a task drops into their
personal assistant queue.

**Step 5 — Close deals faster.**
When your term sheet is accepted, you and the broker close the loan the
traditional way. Byrd & CO stays involved through closing.

### The credit box — what we ask

| Field                | Detail                                                              |
|----------------------|---------------------------------------------------------------------|
| Institution type     | Bank / Credit Union / Private / Agency / Bridge / Hard Money / etc. |
| Property types       | Multifamily, Office, Retail, Industrial, Hospitality, Self-storage, Mixed-use, Medical Office, Mobile Home Park, Land, New Construction |
| Geography            | Comma-separated states, or NATIONWIDE                               |
| Loan size range      | Min and Max dollar amounts                                          |
| Max LTV / LTC        | Percentages                                                         |
| Min DSCR             | Ratio                                                               |
| Min Debt Yield       | Percentage                                                          |
| Rate range           | Min / Max %                                                         |
| Typical term         | Months                                                              |
| Recourse preference  | Recourse / Non-recourse / Either                                    |
| Decision speed       | Days from complete package to term sheet                            |
| Typical fees         | Free-form (e.g. "1% origination, $2,500 processing")                |
| Notes                | Free-form — sweet spots, deal-breakers, personality                 |

The more honest and specific you are, the better we can match. We don't
share your credit box with sponsors.

## For Borrowers

### Why Byrd & CO?

- **One relationship, many lenders.** Instead of shopping your deal to 15
  banks yourself, Byrd & CO does it in parallel — with structured tracking
  so you can see who's engaged, who's countered, who's passed.
- **Guided packaging.** Ada, our AI document concierge, walks you through
  building your PFS, resume, sponsor bio, business plan, proforma, letter
  of explanation, and rent roll — inside your portal. Then attaches them
  where they belong.
- **Multi-sponsor deals handled correctly.** If your deal has more than one
  guarantor (anyone with ≥20% ownership), each sponsor gets their own
  document portal and signs their own fee agreement. Personal docs stay
  private to that sponsor; property docs are shared. No more mixed piles.
- **Real-time term sheet visibility.** When lenders submit term sheets, you
  see them in your portal with rate, loan amount, LTV, term, recourse — and
  the broker's note whenever a term sheet is accepted, countered, or passed.

### The borrower flow

**Step 1 — Meet with a broker.**
Wayne or Caleb sets up your scenario with property, loan request, financials,
and every sponsor on the deal.

**Step 2 — Log in to your portal.**
You get an activation email. Set your password. Inside you'll find:
- A **doc checklist** for every scenario you're on
- Personal docs private to you (PFS, tax returns, resume)
- Shared property/business docs (T-12, rent roll, PSA)
- Ada AI Concierge for help drafting anything
- A signed fee agreement flow (in-portal or by email — legally binding)

**Step 3 — Upload docs.**
Drag-and-drop into the checklist. Or ask Ada to build them for you — she
generates PDFs and files them automatically. Broker reviews and marks each
line as Reviewed, Rejected (with notes on why), or Uploaded.

**Step 4 — Watch term sheets come in.**
Broker shops your deal to matched lenders. Under each scenario in your portal,
a **Lender Term Sheets** section shows every submission — rate, loan amount,
LTV, term, status. When a broker counters or accepts on your behalf, their
note is visible to you so you always know exactly where things stand.

**Step 5 — Close.**
Broker walks you through choosing a lender, executing docs, and closing.
Everything from the marketing package to the executed term sheet lives in
one place.

### What makes multi-sponsor deals easier

- **Ownership % is captured up front.** Anyone with ≥20% ownership is
  automatically flagged as a guarantor.
- **Every guarantor gets their own portal login.** They log in with their
  own email, see only their own personal docs, and upload their own PFS.
- **Passive investors (no signing on the loan) don't need portal access.**
  The broker uploads what's needed on their behalf.
- **Fee agreements are per-sponsor.** Each guarantor signs their own. The
  Certificate of Completion (with IP, timestamp, and ESIGN/UETA compliance
  language) is generated automatically.
